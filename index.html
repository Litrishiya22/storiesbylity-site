About

Small books, read slowly

Storiesbylity started with one idea: a bedtime story should feel like a small, cozy world — not a lesson. Every story is written to be read aloud in one sitting, with room for a child to ask questions along the way.

Each one is written and illustrated in-house, then released as a print book and a digital read-aloud, so families can pick whichever fits the night.
